History

How the Republic of Saturn came to be.

The Republic of Saturn is a clan created on August 10, 2022 by 9aze, and later came to 6b6t on May 30, 2023. The clan is most known for their excessive botting of 6b6t. It's current leader is Maximum4923.

Pre-History

Saturn was originally a social/gaming group called Saturn Empire made by aze that would run it's own Minecraft servers but it was deleted in September 2022 but the friends from back then stayed in touch and came to 6b6t in May 2023.

Classical Saturn (May-December 2023)

Between May 25 and May 30 2023, Vulgur would share screenshots of himself playing 6b6t in a group chat, leading to W1ldy and aze joining him on 6b6t and creating a clan named "Saturn 6b6t" on the server.
Saturn then started to use the IKEA v5 Dupe and also created Saturn's first base - later called Saturn Original Base - and grew the clan to ~5 recruits before leaking the coordinates to the base. They created their stash in the End whilst abusing the IKEA v5 Dupe to create a massive stash - which was later self-griefed by Fulgur. They then created adbots to grow their new server by promising free kits, which eventually led to Fulgur creating a kitbot to automate kit deliveries and further growing the clan.
Following one of the leaders of Shulkergang, bd71legend, ragebaiting and spamming in the Saturn discord, Saturn declared war on Shulkergang.
In June 2023, Saturn griefed all of JaponAce's bases and stashes due to W1ldy insiding the clan. Eventually one of the owners of JaponAce, goxdev, joined Saturn. Saturn created its newest main base and invited ~20 members there. It was eventually self-griefed due to an insider, goxdev (whos previous clan, JaponAce, was insided and griefed by Saturn), attempting to join LagGang in exchange for Saturn's base around August.
In mid-2023, Saturn would go to war with Zealandia and blocking all of their overworld highways, which led to Zealandia spending most of it's time repairing them leading to inactivity.
On July 21, 2023, Advik555 would leak his password to Saturn, resulting in Saturn members griefing all his homes.
On July 22, 2023, Saturn hosted a vanilla pvp tournament with the winner getting a VIP rank, deeznuts69 would eventually become the recipient of this rank.
On July 29, 2023, Saturn participated in the 5th incursion against TheHorizonMC together with Monopoly.
This led Saturn to purge most of their members leading to inactivity, Saturn would go on to build one last main base before growing inactive. Fulgur and aze would leave the Saturn discord around the end of 2023, and W1ldy gave the main base and Saturn discord to bd71legend for his newest clan called Tesseract.
In late 2023, when Saturn was inactive, aze created a new cracked anarchy server called Oxploit, which peaked at 18 players before shutting down. W1ldy and Fulgur backdoored the server with a fake ClearLag plugin which was sent to aze, both W1ldy and Fulgur were later given Admin to help aze grow the server.

Saturnfall (July 2024-August 2025)

Saturn attempted to rejoin 6b6t in July 2024 but was short-lived and eventually died off. In this time, Saturn created 6b6tDB, a website that allowed users to view the live 6b6t chat and also historical chat events.
In December, Saturn made a new private discord with aze, Fulgur, W1ldy, and bd71legend called Saturn Apostles, which ended up leading to the creation of Lambda in February 2025. During lambda, Saturn would run bots to map spawn, develop a mobile client for 6b6tDB, and discover the Nautilus exploit which allowed Saturn members to track every wither spawn and end portal opening. Lambda also saw the construction of a new stash and main base, which would all be griefed by Fulgur in April 2025.

2025 Conflict

After aze ragebaited Fulgur, Fulgur blew up all Saturn/Lambda bases in April 2025, leading to aze deleting the Lambda discord. A month later aze would re-use the July 2024 Saturn discord for a new public Saturn revival, recruiting xqyet, fanlimdk, Vern, MORTEX8, bd71legend, Bocinitaa and 00_Shaurya to help him. Fulgur and Maximum4923 joined The Imperials, which angered aze even more prompting him to ask help from bd71legend to get Fulgur to rejoin Saturn.
aze gave ownership of Saturn to Bocinitaa who renamed it to Paradoxium, aze and Fulgur disliked this so aze tasked mortex to ask bocinitaa for ownership back which he did, then mortex deleted the Saturn discord, and Fulgur, GVD_GAMING and 00_Shaurya griefed Paradoxium's bases.
bd71legend made a deal with Fulgur that he would inside the Imperials for him if bd71legend managed to grief his Imperial recruit base before May 2025, otherwise bd71legend would pay Fulgur $100. A few days later W1ldy teleported aze and bd71legend on the alt wildydev to the recruit base, who later griefed the base.
Fulgur insided the Imperials until Protest17309 gave bd71legend most of the Imperial bases including Elysium, which were griefed on May 6, 2025. The Saturn leaders then co-created a griefing group on 6b6t called NAC, with bd71legend as the leader. NAC died after aze and Fulgur grew sick of bd71legend's personality and gave the Nautilus exploit away to TheKingHastur and Xymb.

Ascendant Saturn (August 2025-Now)

In November 2025, a Discord server was created to announce Saturn's botting of 6b6t to a record player count of 712 players with hundreds of .SaturnXXXX bots online.
Saturn developed the Saturn Hyperloop (also called the infinite homes project) in November 2025, to have thousands of homes available to any of their friends, using their excessive amount of cracked accounts.
In December 2025, Saturn's Christmas base won the 6b6t banner competition becoming the discord's banner for 2 months and appearing in the 2025 Christmas lobby as a mapart.
Saturn repeatedly botted the server, getting a higher and higher playercount each time, until Xymb banned the bots from joining the server after Saturn started making the bots spam in chat. Days after, qbasty implemented an antispam system where Terminators could mute accounts for breaking the chat rules, however this plan was in the making before the Saturn bots started spamming.
In March 2026, Vulgur would recover the original Saturn discord that was owned by bd71legend, leading to a massive growth and increase in activity of Saturn.
Over the course of early 2026, Vulgur extensively tested with clan systems to try and find a "perfect" clan system leading to the eventual creation of Saturn Hyperion (led by qyroxa & Maximum4923) and Saturn Apostles (led by W1ldy).
qyroxa would nuke Panda Clan and CelestialSpawn in mid-2026.
9aze would leave 6b6t and Saturn in August 2026.
The 7th incursion would be made when Vulgur and Lukus1110 discussed TheHorizonMC planning to stream a second time on 6b6t, leading to Saturn's participation in the incursion.
