2026/10 Senate Elections

29 September 2026

On 29 September 2026, the citizens of the Republic of Saturn voted in the October Saturn Senate elections, the results are as followed;

7 votes for Fulgur, 7 votes for mx, 6 votes for qyro, 5 votes for wildy, 5 votes for ractorix, 2 votes for jo, 2 votes for polly, 1 vote for amanises, 1 vote for MrTopHatMc.
