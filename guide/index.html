Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from new players.

What is Saturn?

Saturn is a representative democracy on an anarchy server. Citizens elect a Senate, which meets regularly and is led by a President.

Where can I read the laws?

The Constitution and other laws are on the Republic Laws page. Changes to them are announced in the news.

Who runs the government?

You can see the current President and Senate on the Officials page.

Where do I get help?

Ask in our Discord. Citizens are happy to help new players.
